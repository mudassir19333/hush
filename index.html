Common questions

How do I restore my subscription?

Open Hush, go to Me and tap Restore purchases, or tap Restore on the paywall. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Hush and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Apple’s guide.

The sound stops when the screen locks.

Hush keeps playing in the background. Check that the sleep timer isn’t set too short, and that the volume and Silent/Focus settings allow audio.

Do the sounds work offline?

Yes. Built-in sounds and the sounds you’ve created play without internet. Creating a new sound needs a connection.

How do I delete a sound I made?

On the Create screen, swipe the sound left and tap Delete. It is deleted from your phone and from our server.
