Common questions

How do I restore my subscription?

Open Hush, go to Me and tap Restore purchases, or tap Restore on the paywall. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Hush and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Apple’s guide.

On Android, open the Google Play app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, choose Hush and tap Cancel subscription. Google’s guide.

The sound stops when the screen locks.

Hush keeps playing in the background. Check that the sleep timer isn’t set too short, and that the volume and Silent/Focus settings allow audio.

Do the sounds work offline?

Yes. Built-in sounds and the sounds you’ve created play without internet. Creating a new sound needs a connection.

How do I delete a sound I made?

On the Create screen, swipe the sound left and tap Delete. It is deleted from your phone and from our server.

Delete your data

Hush doesn't ask for a name, email or password. Everything about your baby (name, age, routine, favourites) stays on your phone, and deleting the app removes it.

The only things kept on our server are the sounds you create, their titles and cover images, linked to an anonymous ID.

  • Delete one creation: open Create, swipe the sound left and tap Delete. The sound and its image are removed from our server straight away.
  • Delete everything: delete each of your creations as above, then uninstall Hush. Nothing else about you is stored on our server; the anonymous ID left behind isn't linked to your name, email or device.

Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat for as long as the law requires for billing and tax.
