Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Hush (“the app”) helps parents play calming sounds for their baby. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What stays on your phone

Your answers during setup (your baby’s name and age stage, sleep struggles), your favourites, mixes, routine, timer and reminder settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account ID. When you first use “Create a sound”, the app signs in anonymously so your creations can be saved to you. No name, email or phone number is involved.
  • Sounds you create. The text you type to describe a sound, the mood and length you pick, and the resulting sound, title and cover image are stored on our server (Supabase) so they appear under “Made by you”.
  • Purchase status. Apple processes all payments. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active; it receives an anonymous ID and your purchase history from Apple. We never see your payment details.

How it is used

Only to run the app: to make and keep your sounds and to unlock Premium. We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. The app has no ads and no analytics.

Service providers

To create a sound, your description is sent to these processors, who handle it on our behalf to return the result:

  • ElevenLabs — generates the sound
  • Google (Gemini) — suggests a short name
  • Cloudflare (Workers AI) — draws the cover image
  • Supabase — stores your creations and anonymous account
  • RevenueCat — checks your subscription

Please don’t type personal information (such as names or addresses) into a sound description.

How long we keep it

Your creations are kept until you delete them in the app (swipe a creation left on the Create screen), which removes the sound and its image from our server. To have your anonymous account and everything linked to it deleted, contact us through the support page.

Children

Hush is designed for parents and caregivers, not for children to use themselves. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions or requests: see the Hush support page.
